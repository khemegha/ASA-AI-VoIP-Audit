## Your VoIP is exposed until it is tested

PBX, SIP trunks, IP phones, SBCs and softphones widen your attack surface. Misconfigurations, default credentials, weak segmentation and topology leaks turn your voice platform into an entry point — for eavesdropping, toll fraud and lateral movement into your network.

## Methodology — NIST SP 800-115

Audits follow the **NIST SP 800-115** framework (complemented by VoIPSA), executed on the **authorized scope only**, from a server connected to the VoIP LAN. **Non-destructive, authorized testing only.**

## What we test

- **Targets:** IP-PBX / Asterisk, IP phones, SBC, softphones
- **Protocols & services:** SIP, SSH, SNMP (support & versions)
- **Security checks:** inter-PBX trust relations, SBC / topology hiding, voice/data segmentation, emergency-call routing, default web credentials — and more.
